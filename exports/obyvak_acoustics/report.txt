Kitchen gable: as-built wool 80 mm, air 97.5 mm (wool fraction 0.45). reactance zero 42 Hz; closed form if the wool is transparent 45 Hz; closed form if the wool is opaque 61 Hz.
Bass-band mean field absorption (31.5–125 Hz) is 0.18. The GKB face is a bass resonator and a midrange reflector: every split is back near zero absorption by a few hundred hertz.
Filling the cavity shortens the room decay past 5% (31.5 Hz -0.238 s (-16.5%, audible); 63 Hz -0.012 s (-1.1%); 125 Hz -0.003 s (-0.4%)). The same more-wool direction holds at half and double resistivity. Fill the cavity: wool 177.5 mm, air 0 mm (+0.84 m³ of wool on this gable). Air only goes the other way (31.5 Hz +0.045 s (+3.1%); 63 Hz +0.073 s (+6.7%, audible); 125 Hz +0.002 s (+0.3%)). Packing the cavity is also the simpler install.
The bass-band ranking and the axial marks agree closely enough that a bass FEM is not justified for this gable.
