# Ground floor source

`ground-floor.yaml` is vendored from [pteasima/yaml-ifc](https://github.com/pteasima/yaml-ifc) at commit `0f96c26` (`samples/ground-floor.yaml`). The walls text is the same as the previous pin `34e1597`: butt joints in `connections`, axes snapped onto those joints. The Python package is vendored at `0f96c26` under `vendor/yaml-ifc/`.

It is the walls-and-openings sample for RD Šíma, 1.NP. Lengths are metres. The schema is that repo's `docs/spec.md`. The Blueprints solid is `models/ground_floor.py`.

`bathroom-1.20.yaml` is the room 1.20 tiling document, adapted from that commit's `samples/bathroom.yaml` into storey coordinates. Floor tiles are ABK Poetry Stone 596×596 and wall tiles are 596×1194 portrait, with a 2 mm wall joint (598 mm plan pitch). The floor grid starts at the drain. Wall vertical joints continue that same grid. The window and the door notch the tiles they cross; a notched tile stays one piece, so a jamb is not a joint. Elevation 0 is the east door jamb on the leaf line, 100 mm north of the inner north face; the floor falls 1.5% from there to the drain and continues into the reveal up to that line. `models/bathroom_120.py` turns that file into the tile, grout, and grate solids on the ground-floor model, and into the plan, elevation, and section plates. The screed (with underfloor heating), the waterproofing membrane, and the suspended ceiling are in the file and are not extruded: their thickness was not measured. Room faces are still the drawing dimensions, not a site measure.

`furnishings.yaml` is the room 1.02 furnishing document from commit `34e1597`. Upstream's sample at `0f96c26` is a generic kitchen and does not replace this file. It has the space `SP-1.02` (`id`, `Name`, `PredefinedType: INTERNAL`), the kitchen boxes in `systemFurniture` (`ObjectType: BaseCabinet`, `TallCabinet`, or `WallCabinet`), the side-by-side and the dishwasher in `electricAppliances`, and the undermount sink in `sanitaryTerminals` (`PredefinedType: SINK`). Other list keys, when a placement is added, are `furniture`, `lightFixtures`, and `coverings`. Door hinge, where the note gives one, is `Pset_CabinetDoor` / `HingeSide` (`L` or `R`); yaml-ifc has no hinge field. `Origin` is a plan point `[x, y]`. `Elevation` is the base Z when it is not zero. The space has no footprint and no height. Solids are the boxes `yaml_ifc` writes, inset 1 mm so flush faces do not coincide. Walls in `ground-floor.yaml` are unchanged. Which red number or outline each box comes from is in the comments of `furnishings.yaml`.
