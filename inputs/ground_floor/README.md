# Ground floor source

Design facts: `docs/design/ground-floor.md`, `docs/design/bathrooms.md`, `docs/design/furnishings.md`.

`ground-floor.yaml` is vendored from [pteasima/yaml-ifc](https://github.com/pteasima/yaml-ifc) at commit `0f96c26` (`samples/ground-floor.yaml`). The walls text is the same as the previous pin `34e1597`: butt joints in `connections`, axes snapped onto those joints. The Python package is vendored at `0f96c26` under `vendor/yaml-ifc/`.

It is the walls-and-openings sample for RD Šíma, 1.NP. Lengths are metres. The schema is that repo's `docs/spec.md`. The Blueprints solid is `models/ground_floor.py`.

`bathroom-1.20.yaml` is the room 1.20 tiling document, adapted from that commit's `samples/bathroom.yaml` into storey coordinates. Floor tiles are ABK Poetry Stone 596×596 and wall tiles are 596×1194 portrait, with a 2 mm wall joint (598 mm plan pitch). The floor grid starts at the drain. Wall vertical joints continue that same grid. The window and the door notch the tiles they cross; a notched tile stays one piece, so a jamb is not a joint. Elevation 0 is the east door jamb on the leaf line, 100 mm north of the inner north face; the floor falls 1.5% from there to the drain and continues into the reveal up to that line. `models/bathroom_120.py` turns that file into the tile, grout, and grate solids on the ground-floor model, and into the plan, elevation, and section plates. The screed (with underfloor heating), the waterproofing membrane, and the suspended ceiling are in the file and are not extruded: their thickness was not measured. Room faces are still the drawing dimensions, not a site measure.

`bathroom-1.07.yaml` is the room 1.07 tiling document (Koupelna: Cersanit Zen 190, a vanity with one top and two basins, walk-in shower). The radon chase projects into the neighbouring room, so the bathroom is a clear 4.500 × 2.100 m rectangle, 9.450 m². The door leaf is on the corridor side of the passage, at y=9.475, so the tiled passage adds 1.000 × 0.675 m. The ZTI table lists 8.91 m²; the tiling uses these faces and does not shrink to that area. The floor joint runs through the door centre, including a 2 mm joint where the grid origin used to butt two tiles. Floor tiles outside the shower are ABK Poetry Stone 596×596. The shower, wall and floor, is 549 | 2 | 549 mm (1100 mm, which reads 550 | 550 with the grout) with the glass on that line. The catalog grate is 1100 mm and fills the opening. The catalog body is 1140 mm; its ears are a note only. Both pre-walls share one face. The tall wall extends 50 mm west of the glass, and the glass runs to the face, up to the 2230 mm soffit. Wall tiles are cut at 2230 mm. Above that is plaster, and above the shower a soffit of the same height. The washbasin pre-wall top is tiled at the 1194 mm joint. Above that shelf the north wall is plaster. The shower niche is a 680×280 mm cavity centred on the 549 joint, tiled on the back, both reveals, the top, and the bottom. The level floor and the shower floor share the door-sill grid, y=9.475, in the north-south direction, so those joints line up. The first grout sits in the passage and the north sliver is 188 mm. The west and east walls use that same module. The shower stays 549 mm across the opening. Basin mixers are Paffoni Tango, rozteč 150 mm. The vanity is centred on the door-centre joint. The outer pipes sit on the joints one tile either side of that joint; those joints measure 1195 mm, not 1200, so the mixer centres are 1045 mm apart, not 1050. The vanity is 1600 mm. The shower mixer stays on the central 549 joint, as drawn. The door is 2100 mm high; the passage is tiled to 2230 mm. The shower falls 1.25% from the south entry to a KOV 3G110CM wall drain. Stainless profiles take that fall on both sides. Build-up at finished-floor zero is 250 mm. Under the shower the EPS is 60+40 and the screed at zero is 79 mm. The level floor stays EPS 60+60 and screed 59 mm. `models/bathroom_107.py` turns the file into the tiles, the drain, the fixtures, and the plan plus one elevation per wall.

`bedroom-1.11.yaml` is the room 1.11 (Ložnice) field devices: lights, momentary buttons, the mmWave and the contacts, the data outlet, the ceiling access point, and the blind actuators. The 2D plan is the position, and the 3D origins follow it. Cable number and panel are `Pset_YamlIfc` on each box. The id is `C<cable>-<code>`. Boxes are in the room; there is no back box, wall host, panel, or cable route. The clear room is the L in `docs/electrical/rooms/1.11-geometry.md`, 22.98 m².

`furnishings.yaml` is the room 1.02 furnishing document. The kitchen is the house file from commit `34e1597`; upstream's sample at `0f96c26` is a generic kitchen and does not replace it. The living room (plan v3) is in the same file: the north base and wall cabinets, the bookshelf, the Harvey sofa, the Gatta table and chairs, the rug, the television, and the subwoofer. The kitchen boxes are unchanged. It has the space `SP-1.02` (`id`, `Name`, `PredefinedType: INTERNAL`), the kitchen and living cabinets in `systemFurniture` (`ObjectType: BaseCabinet`, `TallCabinet`, `WallCabinet`, or `FillerCabinet`), the side-by-side, the dishwasher, the television, and the subwoofer in `electricAppliances`, the undermount sink in `sanitaryTerminals` (`PredefinedType: SINK`), the sofa, bookshelf, table, and chairs in `furniture`, and the rug in `coverings` (`PredefinedType: FLOORING`). `lightFixtures` stays unused. Door hinge, where the note gives one, is `Pset_CabinetDoor` / `HingeSide` (`L` or `R`); yaml-ifc has no hinge field. A television is `PredefinedType: USERDEFINED` with `ObjectType: Television`, because this pin has no audiovisual class. `Origin` is a plan point `[x, y]`. `Elevation` is the base Z when it is not zero. The space has no footprint and no height. Solids are the boxes `yaml_ifc` writes, inset 1 mm so flush faces do not coincide. The table, the chairs, and the bookshelf are drawn inside those boxes. Walls in `ground-floor.yaml` are unchanged. Which red number or outline each box comes from is in the comments of `furnishings.yaml`.

The living-room sofa is a Livom Harvey, overall 4380 × 3780 mm. The west face is x = 25.52 and the east face is the TV wall, x = 29.90. The north run is the 860-deep leg along the cabinets, from the TV wall: chaise 1460 × 860, then 116 × 86, then the corner 116 × 86. The west leg is two 116 × 116. There are six backs, each flush with its seat. The two corner backs are each 1160 mm: the north one is the corner seat’s edge, and the west one is the corner’s 860 side plus a 300 return. The chaise back is 1460 and the south-end west back is 1160. The arms are Livom low armrests, 860 × 660 × 300 mm. Each starts at the outer face of the back and closes the corner: the east arm is y 14.29–15.15 and the south arm is x 25.52–26.38. The low base behind the sofa and the bookshelf both start at the same x = 25.52. The Gatta table is centred between the island’s east face (x = 21.754) and that sofa face, 1333 mm to each. The television is centred at y = 12.700, halfway from the south wall y = 10.25 to the front of the 450 mm north cabinets at y = 15.15. Its north edge, y = 13.815, is 475 mm south of the east arm.
